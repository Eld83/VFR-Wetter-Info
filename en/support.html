Support

For questions, bug reports or feedback about the app, reach us by email.

Contact support by email

Helpful details when reporting an issue

  • Device model and Android version
  • App version
  • Affected airport or location
  • A short description of what happened
  • Optionally, a screenshot

Subscription questions

VFR Wetter offers a 7-day free trial, after which the "VFR Wetter Pro" subscription continues via Google Play Billing. You can cancel anytime directly in the Google Play Store under "Subscriptions" – there you can also see the current price and your next billing date. For questions about charges, feel free to also reach out to us.

Important note

VFR Wetter is a supplementary tool for private flight preparation. The app does not replace an official flight weather briefing, NOTAM/AIP checks, or the decision of the pilot in command.
